I am an Associate Professor of Economics at [Paris School of Business](https://www.psbedu.paris/). I work in applied microeconomics, with a focus on behavioral and experimental economics. I use laboratory, online, and field experiments to study how people form beliefs and make decisions, with applications to environmental sustainability, social norm change, collective decision-making, and cooperation.

My current research studies **causal narratives**, i.e., how people explain economic and social phenomena and how these explanations shape their policy preferences, with an ongoing project on climate change narratives. I also work on prosociality, nudges and their unintended effects, and the economics of attention in digital environments.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Lille. Before joining PSB, I was an Associate Professor at Burgundy School of Business and a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute for Futures Studies (Stockholm).

**Roles**
- Executive board member, [Society for the Advancement of Behavioral Economics (SABE)](https://sabeconomics.org/)
- Core member, BC2 Research Cluster, Paris School of Business
- Affiliated researcher, Laboratory for Agent-Based Social Simulation (LABSS), ISTC–National Research Council, Rome
- Lab manager, LESSAC experimental lab, Dijon
