## Working papers

**Accounting for Italy's North–South divide in trusting behaviour: Experimental evidence from Southern migrants**<br>
with Gianluca Grimalda<br>
<details><summary>Abstract</summary>
Regional economic divides often persist despite free mobility, a puzzle attributed to gaps in trust and cooperation preferences. We study Italy's North–South trust divide to test whether such differences dissolve after migration to a higher-trust region. Using trust-game data from a Northern Italian province, we compare natives, Southern migrants, and other-region migrants: Southern migrants trust 29% less than natives, while other migrants are indistinguishable from them. The gap reflects both more pessimistic beliefs about others' cooperation and lower trustworthiness, with beliefs playing the larger role. Trust does not converge toward natives' levels with time since migration, suggesting persistent cultural traits.
</details>
